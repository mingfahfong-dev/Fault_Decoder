What went wrong on the Prepmaster, in plain words

Open your Prepmaster file with Decoder folder. The decoder reads each machine's settings (STATE.INI, PM_NO, PM_UN) to name every unit and place, then lets you pick which days of DIAGNOSE logs to read.

Already opened the folder once? You can also open just a log folder (e.g. D:\HERZOG\log or Log\HAG) or single DIAGNOSE files – the names are remembered on this computer.

Or drag a log folder, the Prepmaster folder or DIAGNOSE files onto this page. Add the WinCC alarm exports (LONG TERM ARCHIVE LIST, HITLIST, MessagesList .csv) the same way to link alarms to the log.
