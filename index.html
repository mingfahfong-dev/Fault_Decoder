What went wrong on the Prepmaster, in plain words

Open your Prepmaster file with Decoder folder. The decoder reads each machine's settings (STATE.INI, PM_NO, PM_UN) to name every unit and place, then lets you pick which days of DIAGNOSE logs to read.

Already opened the folder once? You can also open DIAGNOSE files on their own – the names are remembered on this computer.

Prepmaster Fault Decoder v3 · Shift days run 06:00–06:00 (day 06–18, night 18–06) · Hover a name to see its unit, node or position code · Repeats of the same fault within 2 minutes count once; faults on one piece of equipment less than 3 minutes apart form one incident · Crews A–D follow the 28-day sportsman roster (2D 2N 5 off · 2D 3N 4 off · 3D 2N 5 off), fitted to the PMLB roster · Unit state codes read as 1 automatic, 2 offline, 4 manual, 8 breakdown.
